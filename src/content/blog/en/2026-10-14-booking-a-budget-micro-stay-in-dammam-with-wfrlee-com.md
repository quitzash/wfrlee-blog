## Why Choose a Micro-Stay in Dammam?

A micro-stay is a booking model that allows you to pay only for the hours you need. Whether you have a 6-hour gap between flights or a quick business meeting in the city, booking by the hour provides unmatched flexibility.

### Benefits of Hourly Stays
*   **Cost-Efficiency:** Pay for 3, 6, or 12 hours instead of a full day rate.
*   **Flexibility:** Check in at your convenience, perfectly synced with your flight schedule.
*   **Privacy:** Enjoy a private shower, a comfortable bed, and high-speed Wi-Fi to catch up on work.
*   **Convenience:** Find locations strategically placed between the airport and Dammam city center.
