A **Dubai stopover** is one of the most common in the world. Dubai International Airport (DXB) is a global crossroads between Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Middle East, and it regularly catches six, ten, and fourteen-hour connections.

Dubai is also one of the easiest cities to leave during a layover — provided your visa and timing line up. Here is how to make the most of a stopover at DXB and where to rest.

## Terminals and transit at DXB

Dubai International has three terminals. Emirates operates mainly from Terminal 3, while Terminal 1 and the budget Terminal 2 handle other carriers.

| Terminal | Main airlines | Transit notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terminal 1 | Many international carriers | Direct Metro access |
| Terminal 2 | Low-cost carriers | Smaller, quieter |
| Terminal 3 | Emirates and partners | Largest, most lounges |

## Do you need a visa to leave DXB?

- **Airside transit.** Many nationalities can remain in the terminal without a visa.
- **Transit visa (48 or 96 hours).** Arranged through your airline for eligible travelers who want to leave.
- **Visa on arrival or tourist visa.** Citizens of many countries receive a free visa on arrival; others can apply in advance.

Always confirm the current rules with your airline before you fly, as they change regularly.

## Getting into the city

The Dubai Metro connects directly to Terminals 1 and 3, and taxis and ride-hailing apps are readily available. Downtown is roughly 20–30 minutes away by car outside peak hours.

Keep a generous buffer: security and immigration at DXB can take a while during the evening departure banks.
