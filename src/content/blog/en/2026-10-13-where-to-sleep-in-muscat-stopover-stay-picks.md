<h2 id="why-choose-a-micro-stay-in-muscat">Why Choose a Micro-Stay in Muscat?</h2>

Traditional hotel bookings require checking in at 3 PM and checking out at 11 AM the next day. If your flight lands at 4 AM and departs at 2 PM, you are paying for a full night you won't use. 

Micro-stays allow you to pay only for the hours you need. By choosing a hotel that offers hourly rates, you get:
* **Cost Efficiency:** Only pay for the time you actually spend resting.
* **Flexibility:** Tailor your check-in time to your specific flight schedule.
* **Convenience:** Refresh, shower, and sleep in a real bed without leaving the vicinity of your transit route.

<h2 id="top-areas-to-rest-near-muscat-airport">Top Areas to Rest Near Muscat Airport</h2>

While you explore your options, consider these primary zones:

### The Seeb District
Located closest to the airport, Seeb is perfect for travelers who want to minimize travel time. You can reach several high-quality hotels within a 10-15 minute taxi ride from the terminal. 

### Al Khuwair & Bousher
If you have a layover exceeding 8 hours, these areas offer a mix of business hotels and local culture. You can easily find properties that allow you to book a stopover hotel in Muscat for a 6-12 hour window, giving you enough time to sightsee if you choose.

<h2 id="frequently-asked-questions">Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)</h2>

### Is it possible to book a stopover hotel in Muscat for just a few hours?
Yes. Many hotels in the vicinity of Muscat International Airport have embraced the hourly-stay model, allowing guests to pay only for the time they occupy the room.

### Do I need a visa to stay at a hotel outside the airport?
This depends on your nationality. Most transit passengers can obtain an e-visa or visa-on-arrival if they intend to leave the transit area. Always verify your status with the Royal Oman Police portal before booking.

### How far are the hotels from the Muscat airport terminal?
Most recommended micro-stay hotels are located in the Seeb or Ghubrah areas, which are typically between 10 to 20 minutes away by taxi from the arrivals gate.
