<h2 id="why-pilgrims-need-micro-stays">Why Pilgrims Need Micro-Stays</h2>

The logistics of Umrah often involve long transit times, delayed flights, or gaps between prayers and rituals. Traditional hotel check-in policies (usually 3:00 PM) often fail to accommodate pilgrims arriving on early morning flights or those who simply need a place to refresh before heading to the Haram. 

Micro-stays allow you to pay only for the time you actually need. Whether it is a 4-hour nap between flights or a place to shower and change before your next destination in [Jeddah](/cities/jeddah/) or [Madinah](/cities/madinah/), flexible bookings provide the perfect solution to maximize your comfort without overpaying for a full night’s stay.

<h2 id="benefits-of-hourly-booking">Benefits of Hourly Booking</h2>

*   **Cost Efficiency:** You only pay for the hours you use.
*   **Flexibility:** Check-in whenever you arrive, rather than sticking to rigid hotel schedules.
*   **Proximity:** Many of our partner locations are situated near major transit hubs and central districts.
*   **Privacy & Comfort:** Access to private rooms, bathrooms, and Wi-Fi to recharge your body and devices before continuing your spiritual journey.
