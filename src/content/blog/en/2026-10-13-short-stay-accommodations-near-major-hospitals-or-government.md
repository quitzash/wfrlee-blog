## The Problem with Traditional Waiting
Waiting for a specialist consultation at a hospital in [Jeddah](/cities/jeddah/) or a government permit in [Riyadh](/cities/riyadh/) often involves long, unpredictable gaps. These gaps prevent you from heading home, but make it impossible to stay focused on work. Relying on coffee shops or public lounges leads to physical fatigue and mental burnout, impacting your efficiency for the second half of your day.

* **Cost Efficiency:** You only pay for the time you actually use.
