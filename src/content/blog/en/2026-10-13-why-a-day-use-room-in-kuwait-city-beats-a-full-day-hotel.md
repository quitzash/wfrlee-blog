## The Problem with Full-Day Bookings

When traveling through Kuwait City, many hotels adhere to rigid check-in and check-out times. If your flight arrives in the morning and departs late at night, or if you are attending a business meeting that ends early, a full-day booking is often a waste of resources. You end up paying for a night you won't sleep in and losing the flexibility to manage your schedule on your own terms.

<a id="what-is-micro-stay"></a>
## What is a Micro-Stay?

A micro-stay is the concept of renting a hotel room by the hour rather than by the night. It allows you to access premium hotel facilities—such as high-speed Wi-Fi, a comfortable bed, private bathrooms, and desk space—for a fraction of the cost of a full-night stay. It is the perfect solution for refreshing before a meeting or catching up on rest during a long layover.
