## 3 Steps to a Relaxing Jeddah Layover

### 1. Identify Your Layover Window
Before leaving the airport, calculate your actual free time. Factor in at least 60–90 minutes for security and customs if you plan to leave the transit area. If your layover is 6 hours or longer, booking a micro-stay near [Jeddah](/cities/jeddah/) is highly recommended to shower, rest, and recharge.

## Top Activities for Short Layovers in Jeddah

If you have a bit of energy left after your nap, Jeddah has plenty to offer:
*   **Al-Balad:** Explore the UNESCO World Heritage site and experience the historic charm of Jeddah’s old town.
*   **Jeddah Corniche:** Take a quick drive to the Red Sea waterfront for a breath of fresh air and a beautiful sunset view.
*   **The King Fahd Fountain:** Catch a glimpse of the world's tallest fountain, especially stunning when illuminated at night.

*Note: Always check your visa requirements before exiting the airport.*

## Practical Tips for Transit Travelers
*   **Pack a "Layover Kit":** Keep toiletries, a change of clothes, and chargers in your carry-on bag for easy access.
*   **Stay Connected:** Use the airport Wi-Fi or pick up a local SIM card to manage your bookings on the go.
*   **Plan Your Transport:** Use reliable ride-sharing apps available in Jeddah to travel between the airport and your micro-stay.
