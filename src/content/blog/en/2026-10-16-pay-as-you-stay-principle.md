## What "pay as you stay" means

Pay as you stay is a simple rule: **your cost follows your time.** Instead of paying for 24 hours of a room you will use for four, you choose a time block and pay for that block.

It suits travelers who need a room for a short period:

- **Layover and transit passengers** who want to sleep or shower between flights.
- **Business travelers** who need a quiet room for calls or a reset before a meeting.
- **Families and pilgrims** who arrive early or leave late and want a place to rest.

If you are searching for a **hotel by the hour** near an airport or city center, this is the model to look for.

## Pay as you stay vs. a full-night hotel booking

| Feature | Pay as you stay (short booking) | Standard full-night booking |

| **Best for** | Layovers, rest stops, meeting gaps | Overnight sleep, multi-day trips |
| **Flexibility** | Pick the time block that fits your schedule | Fixed check-in and check-out times |

## A short practical plan

1. **Note your gap.** Check your flight or meeting times and work out how many free hours you really have.
2. **Add a buffer.** Leave time for transport, security, and check-in. For flights, plan to return to the airport early.

5. **Confirm and keep your details handy.** Save your booking confirmation and the property address.
6. **Check in, rest, and head out.** Leave on time so your stay stays within the hours you paid for.
