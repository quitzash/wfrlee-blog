## Step-by-Step Practical Plan for Transit Passengers

To make the most of your layover near RUH, follow these four simple steps:

1. **Verify Your Visa Requirements:** If you are leaving the international transit area to check in at an airport property, ensure you hold an eligible Saudi Stopover Visa or tourist eVisa.
2. **Collect or Check Luggage:** For connections under 12 hours on a single ticket, your bags are typically checked through to your final destination. Keep essentials in your carry-on.
3. **Select Your Booking Length:** If your transit is less than 8 hours, booking a full 24-hour rate can be unnecessary. Consider flexible booking platforms where you only pay for the exact hours used.
4. **Coordinate Your Return Transfer:** King Khalid International Airport security checkpoints can get busy during peak evening banks. Aim to arrive back at your departure terminal at least 2.5 hours before international flights.
