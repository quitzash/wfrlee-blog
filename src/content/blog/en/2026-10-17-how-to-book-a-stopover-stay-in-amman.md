## Is a stopover stay in Amman worth it?

It usually is when your layover is **4 hours or longer**, or when you land late at night and fly out early. Queen Alia International Airport sits roughly 35 km south of central Amman, so the right choice depends on how much time you really have.

Rule of thumb: if you can't comfortably cover the round trip, boarding buffer, and security, stay near the airport.

## Compare your options

| Option | Best for | Typical layover | Things to know |
|---|---|---|---|
| Airport-area hotel | Short, low-stress rests | 4-8 hours | Closest to AMM; easiest to return for check-in |
| Hourly / day-use room | Sleep, shower, remote work | 3-6 hours | Pay for the hours you need; confirm check-in and check-out times |
| Amman city hotel | Longer stopovers and sightseeing | 8+ hours | Allow roughly 40-60 minutes each way, traffic permitting |

## Highlights to look for

- **Transfer options:** Shuttle, taxi, or ride-hailing, and how long each takes.
- **Flexible hours:** Blocks that match your actual layover, not a fixed night.
- **Quiet rooms and fast Wi-Fi:** Useful for sleeping or working between flights.
- **Luggage handling:** Check whether you can store bags or must carry them.
- **Clear cancellation terms:** Flights change, so confirm the policy before paying.

## A simple 5-step booking plan

1. **Calculate your real window.** Subtract the airport transfer, baggage collection, immigration, and a 2.5-3 hour pre-departure buffer from your layover.
2. **Check entry requirements.** Rules depend on your nationality, ticket, and whether you leave the airport. Verify with your airline or official Jordanian sources before booking.
3. **Choose location by time.** Airport area for shorter windows, city for 8+ hours.
4. **Pick your hours.** Search on [Wfrlee](https://wfrlee.com), enter your arrival and departure times, and select a by-the-hour slot.
5. **Save your details.** Keep the confirmation, hotel address, and contact number handy, and plan your return time before you settle in.
